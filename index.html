EDITORIAL NOTE

A book about being there: a pause between classes, fluorescent reflections, a brief gaze at the blackboard, and gestures that were never arranged.

The photographs keep their original positions and proportions while low light, motion trails, grain, and shadow build one continuous atmosphere.
